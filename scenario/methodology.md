# Investigation Methodology

## Progressive Investigation Approach

This project uses a progressive, evidence-driven investigation methodology.

An internal incident scenario was designed before the investigation began. However, the complete sequence of events is intentionally not disclosed to the investigator in advance.

The investigation will progress through the analysis of the available evidence.

The process will involve:

- Identifying observable facts
- Formulating questions
- Developing hypotheses
- Searching for supporting or contradictory evidence
- Pivoting to new data sources when required
- Distinguishing confirmed findings from assumptions and inferences
- Reconstructing the incident based on the available evidence

## Evidence-Based Conclusions

No conclusion should be considered confirmed without supporting evidence.

During the investigation, findings will be classified according to their evidential status:

- **Confirmed Fact:** directly supported by available evidence.
- **Hypothesis:** a possible explanation that requires validation.
- **Inference:** a reasoned conclusion derived from multiple pieces of evidence.
- **Unconfirmed Information:** information that cannot yet be sufficiently validated.

## Scenario Design Principles

The simulated scenario is designed to include a mixture of:

- Legitimate activity
- Suspicious events
- Operational noise
- Potential false positives
- Ambiguous elements

This approach is intended to simulate a realistic investigation in which the initial alert does not reveal the complete incident story.

## Transparency

The scenario, logs, and events used in this project are simulated for educational and portfolio purposes.

The internal incident narrative is intentionally kept undisclosed during the active investigation to preserve the progressive nature of the analysis.
